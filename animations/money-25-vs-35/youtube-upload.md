THE NUMBERS FROM THE VIDEO
• Sam starts at 25, puts in $48,000 in total, ends with about $262,000 at 65
• Alex starts at 35, puts in $36,000 in total, ends with about $122,000 at 65
• Sam saves only from 25 to 35 and then stops: still about $140,000 at 65
• Alex doubles to $200 a month: about $244,000, still behind Sam
• A 1% yearly fee cuts Sam's $262,000 down to about $199,000

WHAT MAKES A GOOD INDEX FUND
